prose: continue the fluency pass; wire three orphaned figures

Fluency. Whole-manuscript comma density 1.10 -> 1.26 against a 1.5-2.5 target for
fluent academic Chinese. Two mechanisms were used, in this order:

1. Manual rewrites of the worst paragraphs, joining reason to claim with
   而/则/因而/使得 instead of splitting them into separate declaratives. Ch4 was
   the stiffest chapter (1.06) and is now 1.18; its four worst paragraphs went
   from 0.62-0.75 to 1.20-1.75.
2. A mechanical merge of 29 figure lead-ins with the sentence that followed
   them, since that construction was the single largest source of comma-free
   sentences. One merge produced a grammar error (a question mark had been
   turned into a comma) and was reverted by hand.

What was deliberately not done: the manuscript still has 220 comma-free clauses,
but most of them are short deliberate beats that carry the rhythm. Flattening
those to raise a metric would make the prose worse, so they stay.

Figure-text linkage. A check for figures that no paragraph ever references,
outside their own float, found three: fig:ident in ch5 and fig:demo1 /
fig:demoopen in ch6. All three now have a sentence that says what they show.
Orphan count is zero.

77 pages, 35 tests, 15/15 log checks, PDF and DOCX rebuilt.
