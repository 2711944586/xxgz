\chapter*{摘要}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘要}

家用蒸箱靠饱和蒸汽在食品表面冷凝释放潜热，对流烤箱靠干热空气和管壁辐射把表面推到褐变温度。两台机器分体布置既占台面，又各自保留互不引用的定时曲线。并入同一腔体之后，操作者选择的仍是蒸、烤或组合，机器真正能改的只有加热器功率、蒸汽阀、排汽和风机。冷点温度、内部含水和反应进度并不在这四个旋钮上。机械定时、腔温闭环、按重量调用曲线、穿刺探针和表面红外，闭合的是空气温度、申报质量或刺入点；这一次过程里的内部热阻从未进入后验。同一阀门对冷点和表面品质的符号可以相反：为冷点多送一股蒸汽，表面可能被打湿到无法结壳；为表面排湿而加大排汽，冷点又可能失去冷凝潜热。本报告把一体机当作信息感知问题来处理。传感器只能装在腔体侧。稳态观测若压不低决策量的后验不确定度，控制器就必须离开平衡，用短时边界激励把内部参数从灵敏度零的状态里拖出来。家用时域取 \(55\,\mathrm{min}\)。\(15\,\mathrm{mm}\) 的导热时间尺度已经走完，\(50\,\mathrm{mm}\) 的 Fourier 数只有 \(0.12\)，同一套旋钮在薄件和厚件上给出相反的停机符号。

方案分成五层：过程、多尺度模型、估计、对偶决策和安全。过程层给出腔体与食品边界。多尺度模型把多孔介质场压成冷点、表面、进度和热阻。估计层指出稳态观测对内部热阻的灵敏度为零，于是用离开平衡的短时探针激发第二个灵敏度方向。对偶决策把信息动作与品质动作分开。安全层只用水位、门、漏电和加热器温度改写执行量，品质估计不进入断电条件。蒸、烤、组合三档只改变四个执行量的允许集合。

数值核沿厚度用十六条控制体积分，时间步 \(2\,\mathrm{s}\)。主对照 S1 共 \(19440\) 条样本，档位研究 \(11664\) 条，并辅以网格加密、因子消融、宽格子、二维截面和种子 7 轨迹族。开环菜单按厚度给出 \(900/1170/1530\,\mathrm{s}\)，中位 \(e_{\alpha}=-0.843\)。腔温比例闭合空气温度、不停机，能量中位 \(3310\)。对偶策略在 \(150\)--\(190\,\mathrm{s}\) 的两段探针（先蒸汽、后风机）之后，主对照 S1 的 \(4860\) 条样本全部把热阻方差从 \(0.64\) 降到 \(0.224\)，档位研究里只有三分之二完成更新，未更新的全部落在蒸档；档位研究中 \(50\,\mathrm{mm}\)、组合、对偶的中位 \(e_{\alpha}=-0.782\)，全部低于设计线 \(\alpha_m=0.85\)（恒温冷点上 \(F=3.794\,\mathrm{min}\)）。种子 7、肉糜 \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\)、组合、对偶停于 \(1866\,\mathrm{s}\)，\(e_{\alpha}=+0.145\)，能量 \(1864\)。

本报告围绕四个问题组织，每个问题都有日志支撑的答案。其一，降阶的代价有多大：把两节点模型拟合到十六条场，外部膜热阻可恢复到两倍以内，内部热阻与内部热容却落到解析值的 \(23\)--\(363\) 倍与 \(0.35\)--\(8.1\) 倍，因为场上的表面--冷点温差是非单调的，单阶环节画不出这条曲线。其二，一维核丢了什么：同一套边界下一维与二维冷点只差 \(0.31\,\mathrm{K}\)，一维最低格作为决策温度是够用的；\(13.75\,\mathrm{mm}\) 是半厚距离而非横向偏移，冷点是一块占全场 \(8.3\%\) 的平台，位置在温度证据下本就病态。其三，信息动作是否奏效：S1 的对偶 \(4860\) 行全部把方差降下来，档位研究只有三分之二，未更新的全部落在蒸档。其四，瓶颈在估计器还是决策：两状态 EKF 把滞后倍数的偏差从 \(-2.93/-2.88/-4.07\) 收窄到 \(-1.77/-0.58/-1.86\)、把方差从两个取值变成 \(16\)--\(42\) 个，停机误差却没有改善；滚动时域 MPC 在三种厚度上最省能量，却在 \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\) 上因共用有偏估计而提前 \(488\,\mathrm{s}\) 停机。更好的估计器买到的是可读的后验，更好的优化器接在有偏输入后面只会更快地做错事；这两句在这里都是测出来的，不是断言的。

同一状态流读回三维腔体，交互页为 \url{https://2711944586.github.io/xxgz/}。每个日志都记录了两套数值核中的哪一套产生了它，\texttt{scripts/verify\_logs.py} 按代际重算 \(15\) 个抽样格子。

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Two appliances fail in four ways: they take up counter space, steaming is hard to time so the centre ends up undercooked or the surface overcooked, baking leaves temperature and duration uncertain and the heating uneven, and both carry residual-current and overtemperature hazards. A household steamer heats by condensation of saturated vapour on the food surface; a convection oven heats by dry air and tube-wall radiation. Merging both boundaries into one cavity removes the second appliance but leaves the machine with the same four actuators: heater power, steam valve, exhaust and fan. Cold-spot temperature, internal moisture and reaction progress cannot be set by those actuators. Mechanical timers, cavity-temperature loops, weight-indexed menus, puncture probes and surface infrared regulate only air temperature, the declared mass, or the temperature at a single puncture point; the internal thermal resistance of this particular piece never enters the posterior. The same valve can carry opposite signs for the cold spot and for surface quality. This report treats the appliance as an information-sensing problem. Only cavity-side sensors can be installed. When steady observations cannot bring the posterior uncertainty of the decision functional within tolerance, the controller must depart from equilibrium and excite the boundary so that the internal parameter leaves its zero-sensitivity state. The household horizon is \(55\,\mathrm{min}\). A \(15\,\mathrm{mm}\) piece has already finished its conduction scale, while a \(50\,\mathrm{mm}\) piece sits at Fourier number \(0.12\); the same four actuators therefore produce opposite stop signs.

The architecture has five layers: process, multi-scale model, estimator, dual decision and safety. At equilibrium the three resistance and capacity parameters drop out of the algebra, so steady observation carries no information about them. A two-stage probe in a \(40\,\mathrm{s}\) window---steam first, fan second---supplies a second, linearly independent sensitivity direction. Dual decision separates the information action from the quality action. The safety layer rewrites the commands using only water level, door position, residual current and heater temperature; quality estimates never enter the cut-off. Steam, bake and combi modes restrict only the admissible set of the same four actuators.

The study is organised around four questions that each have a logged answer. First, what does the reduced model cost? Fitting a two-node reduction to the sixteen-cell field recovers the external film resistance to within a factor of \(2.3\), but the internal resistance and the internal capacity land \(23\)--\(363\) and \(0.35\)--\(8.1\) times their analytic values, because the field's surface-to-core gap is non-monotone and a single lag cannot reproduce it. Second, how much does the 1D kernel lose? Under one boundary law the 1D and 2D cold spots differ by \(0.31\,\mathrm{K}\), so the lowest-cell reading is a sound decision temperature; the \(13.75\,\mathrm{mm}\) figure is a half-thickness, not a lateral offset, and the cold spot is a plateau spanning \(8.3\%\) of the field at both grid resolutions. Third, does the information action work? On the S1 lattice of \(19440\) samples all \(4860\) dual rows reduce the resistance variance from \(0.64\) to \(0.224\), while only two thirds of the \(11664\)-row cook-mode study do and every miss sits in the steam mode, where the heat cap flattens the probe. Fourth, is the estimator or the decision rule the bottleneck? A two-state extended Kalman filter cuts the lag-multiplier bias from \(-2.93/-2.88/-4.07\) to \(-1.77/-0.58/-1.86\) at \(15/30/50\,\mathrm{mm}\) and turns the posterior variance from two discrete values into \(16\)--\(42\), yet the stopping error does not improve; a rolling-horizon MPC spends the least energy at every thickness but stops \(488\,\mathrm{s}\) early at \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\) because it optimises on the same biased input. A better estimator buys a legible posterior, and a better optimiser behind a biased input only errs faster; both statements are measured here rather than asserted.

The open-loop menu writes \(900/1170/1530\,\mathrm{s}\) by thickness and returns median \(e_{\alpha}=-0.843\). Cavity-temperature proportion never stops and spends median energy \(3310\). Seed 7, mince, \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\), combi, dual stops at \(1866\,\mathrm{s}\) with \(e_{\alpha}=+0.145\) and energy \(1864\). Every log records which of the two kernel generations produced it, and \(15\) sampled cells are re-derived by \texttt{scripts/verify\_logs.py}. The same logged state is read back into a three-dimensional cavity at \url{https://2711944586.github.io/xxgz/}.
