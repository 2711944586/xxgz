\chapter*{摘要}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘要}

家用蒸烤一体机把蒸箱与烤箱并进同一腔体，省下的是台面；没有省下的，是冷点温度、内部含水与反应进度这组真正决定熟度的量。机器可动的只有加热、蒸汽、排汽与风机四个执行量，而公开方案中常见的机械定时、腔温闭环、按重量调用曲线、穿刺探针和表面红外，闭合的主要是空气温度、申报质量或局部读数；由主动边界激励在线估计这一次过程内部热阻的方法尚未在所查资料中披露。同一阀门对冷点与表面品质的符号可以相反，符号冲突因此无法回避。本文把一体机写成信息感知问题，给出四层结果。

\textbf{不可辨识的根源与信息解。}平衡点上，两节点降阶方程中 \((R_{\mathrm{ext}},R_{\mathrm{int}},C_1,C_2)\) 只以除数出现，稳态观测对其灵敏度为零；三个内部热阻在平衡段给出的冷点差小于 \(0.01\,\mathrm{K}\)。信息只能从离开平衡的激励中取得。据此设计 \(150\)--\(190\,\mathrm{s}\) 的两段探针：先蒸汽改相变符号以离开平衡，后风机改外部热阻以改善条件数。单一时刻的标量观测最多提供秩 \(1\)，沿时间堆叠的累计灵敏度矩阵在独立激励下可达满列秩；第二段把两个参数方向的张角拉大、条件数压低——两段各做一件不同的事。当前评分核用由真值温差构成的仿真评价参照，因此探针更新结果定位为仿真级概念验证。

\textbf{降阶的代价。}把两节点模型拟合到一维场，外部膜热阻可恢复到解析值的 \(2.3\) 倍以内，内部热阻却落到 \(23\)--\(363\) 倍。残差是结构性的：场上的表面--冷点温差先张开到 \(108\,\mathrm{K}\) 再收拢到零，而单阶滞后只有一个指数逼近，画不出这条双相曲线。降阶因此是"可收缩到决策状态"，不是轨迹级等价。

\textbf{策略的几何。}把厚度放开成 \(10\)--\(60\,\mathrm{mm}\)、初温放开成 \(2\)--\(26^\circ\mathrm{C}\) 的连续参数后，四条策略的设计线沿厚度轴依次右移：腔温比例 \(16\,\mathrm{mm}\)，开环 \(20\)--\(25\,\mathrm{mm}\)，无探针 \(30\)--\(35\,\mathrm{mm}\)，对偶在 \(30\)--\(45\,\mathrm{mm}\) 摊成平台。对偶的可用厚度窗口最宽，这是它的核心增益。同一平面上含水轴因活度饱和完全退化，二十级取值只给一个结果。

\textbf{瓶颈的分离。}两状态 EKF 把滞后倍数偏差从 \(-2.93/-2.88/-4.07\) 收窄到 \(-1.77/-0.58/-1.86\)，后验方差从两个取值变成 \(16\)--\(42\) 个，停机偏差却没有改善；滚动时域 MPC 在三种厚度上的累计加热指令最小，却在 \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\) 上因共用有偏估计而提前 \(488\,\mathrm{s}\) 停机。更好的估计器买到可读的后验，更好的优化器接在有偏输入后面只会更快地做错事。

数值核用十六条控制体，时间步 \(2\,\mathrm{s}\)。主对照 \(19440\) 条样本，档位研究 \(11664\) 条，连续参数曲面 \(8.2\times 10^{4}\) 次积分。开环中位 \(e_{\alpha}=-0.843\)，对偶在 \(30\,\mathrm{mm}\) 中位 \(-0.080\)，种子 7 组合档对偶停于 \(1866\,\mathrm{s}\)、\(e_{\alpha}=+0.145\)。本文的主要验证范围是内部热阻辨识与冷点热力进度控制；主对照中的褐变进度接近零，累计加热指令指标不等同于实际电能，安全层也只完成了仿真级接口验证。所有日志记录代际，抽样格子由脚本复算。

\noindent\textbf{关键词：} 信息感知；蒸烤一体机；逆热传导；对偶控制；控制屏障；食品热加工

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

A combi steamer merges two appliances into one cavity and saves counter space. What it does not save is the group of quantities that actually decide doneness: cold-spot temperature, internal moisture and reaction progress. The machine can only move four actuators---heater, steam valve, exhaust and fan---while the public configurations examined here close air temperature, declared mass or a local probe reading. The reviewed material does not disclose an online method that estimates the internal thermal resistance of the particular piece through active boundary excitation, and the same valve can carry opposite signs for the cold spot and for surface quality. This paper treats the appliance as an information-sensing problem and reports four results.

\textbf{The algebraic root of unidentifiability, and its information solution.} At equilibrium the two-node reduction contains $(R_{\mathrm{ext}},R_{\mathrm{int}},C_1,C_2)$ only as divisors, so steady observation has exactly zero sensitivity to them; numerically, three different internal resistances produce cold spots within $0.01\,\mathrm{K}$ of each other throughout the equilibrium phase. Information can only be bought by leaving equilibrium. A two-stage probe in a $150$--$190\,\mathrm{s}$ window therefore does two distinct things: steam first, to change the phase-change sign and leave equilibrium; then the fan, to change the external resistance. A scalar observation at one time has rank at most one, whereas the time-stacked sensitivity matrix can reach full column rank when the excitation directions are independent. The fan stage widens the angle between those directions and lowers the condition number. The score kernel uses a truth-temperature proxy for the update acceptance, so this result is a simulation-level proof of concept.

\textbf{The price of reduction.} Fitting the two-node model to the sixteen-cell field recovers the external film resistance to within a factor of $2.3$, but the internal resistance lands $23$--$363$ times its analytic value. The residual is structural: the surface-to-core gap opens to $108\,\mathrm{K}$ and then closes to zero, while a single first-order lag has one exponential approach and cannot draw that two-phase curve. The reduction is therefore a contraction to the decision state, not a trajectory-faithful model.

\textbf{The geometry of the strategies.} Opening thickness to $10$--$60\,\mathrm{mm}$ and initial temperature to $2$--$26\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ as continuous axes shows the design line of each policy shifting right along the thickness axis: cavity-temperature proportion at $16\,\mathrm{mm}$, open loop at $20$--$25\,\mathrm{mm}$, no-probe at $30$--$35\,\mathrm{mm}$, and dual spreading into a $30$--$45\,\mathrm{mm}$ plateau. The dual policy therefore has the widest usable thickness window, which is its core gain. On the same plane the moisture axis is fully degenerate, because activity saturates: twenty levels return a single outcome.

\textbf{Separating the estimator bottleneck from the decision bottleneck.} A two-state extended Kalman filter narrows the lag-multiplier bias from $-2.93/-2.88/-4.07$ to $-1.77/-0.58/-1.86$ at $15/30/50\,\mathrm{mm}$ and turns the posterior variance from two discrete values into $16$--$42$, yet the stopping error does not improve. A rolling-horizon MPC uses the least cumulative heating command at every thickness but stops $488\,\mathrm{s}$ early at $30\,\mathrm{mm}$ because it optimises on the same biased estimate. A better estimator buys a legible posterior; a better optimiser behind a biased input only errs faster.

The kernel integrates sixteen control volumes at a $2\,\mathrm{s}$ step. The main lattice has $19440$ samples, the cook-mode study $11664$, and the continuous surfaces $8.2\times 10^{4}$ integrations. Open loop returns median $e_{\alpha}=-0.843$; dual reaches $-0.080$ at $30\,\mathrm{mm}$; seed 7, combi, dual stops at $1866\,\mathrm{s}$ with $e_{\alpha}=+0.145$. The validated scope is internal-resistance inference and cold-spot progress control: browning is nearly zero in the main comparison, cumulative heating is a command integral rather than electric energy, and the safety layer is verified at simulation-interface level. Every log records which kernel generation produced it, and sampled cells are re-derived by script.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} information sensing; combi steamer; inverse heat transfer; dual control; control barrier; food thermal processing
