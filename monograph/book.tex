\chapter*{摘要}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘要}

家用蒸箱把饱和蒸汽送进封闭腔体，靠表面冷凝释放潜热，把热量从外向内送进食品。对流烤箱走另一条路：干热空气和管壁辐射把表面推到褐变温度，内部仍靠导热慢慢跟上。两台机器分体布置既占台面，又各自保留一套互不引用的定时曲线。蒸制时蒸汽量大、时间难校准，中心未熟与表面过湿常常一起出现。烤制这边更常见的是腔温波动、搁架与门缝造成的边界不对称，内部冷点往往偏离几何中心。漏电、干烧和超温是两类机器共有的电气热保护问题。现有保护回路读取水位、门开关、剩余电流和加热器温度，与食品内部的进度估计分属两套回路。

把蒸和烤并入同一腔体，台面占用的问题当场消失。并入之后，操作者在面板上选择的仍是蒸、烤或组合，机器真正能改的仍只有四个量：加热器功率、蒸汽阀、排汽和风机。食品一旦进门，冷点温度、内部含水和反应进度都不在这四个旋钮上。机械定时、腔温闭环、按重量调用曲线、穿刺探针和表面红外，闭合的是空气温度、申报质量或刺入点温度。这一次过程里的内部热阻从未进入后验。蒸汽把表面打湿、加热把表面推干，同一阀门对冷点和表面品质的符号可以相反。本文以信息感知为主线：在可安装观测之下，决策泛函的剩余不确定度若仍高于允许误判，执行器必须离开平衡去购买信息。四个边界写成表面通量，内部不可见量写成九维决策状态，同一腔体上给出可以比较的感知与控制方案。家用时域取 55 min。15 mm 的导热时间尺度已经走完，50 mm 的 Fourier 数只有 0.12，同一套旋钮在薄件和厚件上给出相反的停机符号。

方案按五层组织。过程层给出腔体与食品的边界；多尺度模型把多孔介质场压缩为冷点、表面、进度和热阻；估计层说明稳态观测对内部热阻的灵敏度为零，因而用离开平衡的短时探针购买可辨识性；对偶决策把信息动作与品质动作分开；安全层只用水位、门、漏电和加热器温度改写执行量，品质估计不进入断电条件。蒸、烤、组合三档只改变四个执行量的允许集合，食品模块收到的是通量。

数值核沿厚度用十六条控制体积分，时间步 \(2\,\mathrm{s}\)，时域 55 min。主对照 S1 包含三种食品、三种厚度、两种初温、三种含水、三种非均质和四种策略，每个格子 30 个种子，共计 19440 条样本。另有一份 11664 条的档位研究，以及网格加密、因子消融、宽格子、二维截面和种子 7 轨迹族。开环菜单按厚度给出 900/1170/1530 s，薄件被推到进度上沿、厚件停在设计线之下，中位 \(e_{\alpha}=-0.843\)。腔温比例闭合空气温度，不停机，能量中位 3310。对偶策略在 150--190 s 的两段探针之后，约三分之二的样本把热阻方差从 0.64 降到 0.224；档位研究里 50 mm、组合、对偶的中位 \(e_{\alpha}=-0.782\)，全部低于设计线。设计线 \(\alpha_m=0.85\) 对应恒温冷点上 \(F=3.794\,\mathrm{min}\)，用作策略比较的零点。

配套交互演示读取同一数值核写出的状态流，在三维腔体中同时显示四个边界、内部温度色带、因果路径的显示量和安全投影前后的加热。腔体按家用蒸烤箱的可见零件建模：六根带陶瓷端帽的 U 形加热管、十叶深色扭曲风机（叶片在护网前方、后有近黑挡板和电机罩）、双层门玻璃、带冲孔的烤盘、蒸汽喷嘴与约 720 个粒子、七片排汽百叶、十六块几乎贴合的食品薄片（第 5 片为脂肪夹层）。正文公式编号以 PDF 为准，并提供同一正文的 Word 文本。封面与页眉标明学号 20241334、姓名庄颂、班级信息2402。

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

A household steamer delivers heat by condensation of saturated vapour on the food surface; a convection oven delivers it by dry air and tube-wall radiation. Putting both boundaries into one cavity answers the bench-space question and leaves the machine with the same four actuators it already had: heater power, steam valve, exhaust and fan. Once the door is closed, cold-spot temperature, internal moisture and reaction progress sit behind those four knobs. Mechanical timers, cavity-temperature loops, weight-indexed menus, puncture probes and surface infrared close air temperature, declared mass or a puncture-point reading. Internal thermal resistance of this particular piece never enters the posterior. The report treats the appliance as an information-sensing problem: if residual uncertainty of the decision functional still exceeds the allowed misclassification under installable observations, the actuators must leave equilibrium to buy information.

The architecture has five layers. The process layer supplies cavity and food boundaries. The multi-scale model compresses the porous-medium field into cold spot, surface, progress and resistance. The estimator shows that steady observations have zero sensitivity to internal resistance, so a two-stage probe in a 40 s window purchases a second sensitivity direction. Dual decision separates the information action from the quality action. The safety layer rewrites commands using only water level, door, residual current and heater temperature; quality estimates never enter the cut-off. Steam, bake and combi modes only restrict the admissible set of the same four actuators. The household horizon is 55 min: a 15 mm piece already finishes its conduction scale, a 50 mm piece remains at Fourier number 0.12, and the same four knobs therefore yield opposite stop signs on thin and thick pieces.

A one-dimensional kernel with sixteen control volumes, time step \(2\,\mathrm{s}\) and horizon 55 min scores four policies on the S1 lattice of 19440 samples (three foods, three thicknesses, two initial temperatures, three moistures, three heterogeneities, four policies, thirty seeds). A 11664-row cook-mode study, grid refinement from 8 to 32 volumes, factor ablation, a 24-by-16 explicit section and the seed-7 family sit beside that table. The open-loop menu writes 900/1170/1530 s by thickness and returns median \(e_{\alpha}=-0.843\). Cavity-temperature proportion never stops and spends median energy 3310. After the dual probe, about two thirds of samples reduce resistance variance from 0.64 to 0.224; on 50 mm combi dual the cook-mode median is \(e_{\alpha}=-0.782\), entirely below the design line \(\alpha_m=0.85\) (\(F=3.794\,\mathrm{min}\) at a constant cold spot). Seed 7, mince, 30 mm, combi, dual stops at 1866 s with \(e_{\alpha}=+0.145\).

An interactive cavity reads the same logged state and shows six U-tube heaters with ceramic caps and chrome bus bars, a ten-blade dark twisted fan in front of a sparse grille and a near-black backplate, double-pane door glass, a punched tray, steam nozzle with about 720 particles, seven exhaust louvres and sixteen nearly contiguous food slabs with a fat layer at index 5. Cover and running header carry student number 20241334, name Zhuang Song and class Information 2402.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} information sensing; combi steamer; inverse heat transfer; dual control; control barrier; food thermal processing
